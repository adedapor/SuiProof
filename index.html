How it works

01

Upload. Or paste.

Drop a file — PDF, image, doc, anything — or just paste raw text. SuiProof handles both.

02

Hash. Seal.

Your document is hashed with SHA-256 and stored on Walrus, a decentralized storage network built on Sui. Permanent and tamper-evident.

03

Get your proof link.

SuiProof generates a verification link containing your blob ID and hash. Anyone with it can verify your document, anytime.

04

Share. Forever.

No wallet. No crypto knowledge. No backend. Just a link that proves your document existed, unchanged, at that moment in time.
